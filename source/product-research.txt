DELIVERY REFERENCE — MEXICO
Belcorp Mexico terms consulted for this interface state that order preparation occurs after campaign close and list delivery periods for Mexico. The interface therefore labels its date range as an INFORMATIONAL ESTIMATE rather than a guaranteed delivery promise. The build uses a 3–11 business-day reference for the rest of Mexico.
